The life

Born into comfort, trained as a doctor, made by the road. Here is the arc.

1928

Rosario, Argentina

Ernesto Guevara is born on 14 June. Asthma shapes his childhood and pushes him toward books, chess and rugby.

1953

Doctor

He qualifies in medicine at the University of Buenos Aires, then sets out again across the continent.

1954

Guatemala

He witnesses the US-backed coup against President Árbenz, which hardens his views on imperialism.

1955

Mexico City

He meets Fidel Castro and joins the plan to overthrow Batista.

1959–64

In government

President of the National Bank, then Minister of Industries. He travels the world as Cuba's envoy.

1967

Bolivia

He is captured on 8 October and executed the next day, aged 39.
