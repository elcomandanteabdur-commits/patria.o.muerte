The last campaign

In 1965 he left Cuba to export revolution. It did not go as planned.

1965

Congo

He fights alongside Congolese rebels and withdraws after about seven months, disillusioned.

Nov 1966

Bolivia

He arrives in disguise to build a guerrilla force. Local support never comes.

8 Oct 1967

Quebrada del Yuro

Bolivian soldiers, advised by the CIA, capture him.

9 Oct 1967

La Higuera

He is executed. His body is hidden.

1997

Found

His remains are discovered, moved to Cuba and laid in a mausoleum in Santa Clara.
