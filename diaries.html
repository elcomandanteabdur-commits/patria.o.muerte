The ride

In late 1951 Ernesto and his friend Alberto Granado left Argentina on a worn-out Norton 500 nicknamed La Poderosa. The bike died along the way. The trip did not.

Dec 1951

Buenos Aires to the Andes

They head south and west, living on favours and odd jobs.

1952

Chile

The motorcycle gives up. They continue on foot, by truck and by raft.

Jun 1952

San Pablo leper colony, Peru

Weeks working alongside patients leave a deep mark on both men.

Jul 1952

Colombia and Venezuela

Ernesto flies home. He is now a different man from the one who left.
