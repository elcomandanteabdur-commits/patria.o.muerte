Cuba

Eighty-two men crowded onto the yacht Granma. Two years later Batista was gone.

2 Dec 1956

Granma lands

Most of the expedition is killed or captured. A handful reach the Sierra Maestra.

1957

Guerrilla war

Ernesto, the group's doctor, becomes a commander. He also runs a radio station and a school.

Dec 1958

Santa Clara

He leads the attack and derails an armoured train. The city falls.

1 Jan 1959

Batista flees

Che's column enters Havana days later.

1961

Minister of Industries

He pushes rapid industrialisation and voluntary labour. Results are mixed.
